## 📚 语法 Grammar

### 1. 现在进行时 (The Present Continuous Tense)

表示**此时此刻正在发生**的动作，结构为 **be + 动词-ing**。

- 肯定句：`主语 + be (am/is/are) + doing`
  - `They are walking over the bridge.`
- 否定句：在 `be` 后加 `not`
  - `The sun is not shining.`
- 一般疑问句：把 `be` 提到句首
  - `Is the ship going under the bridge?`

动词加 `-ing` 的规则：

| 规则 | 示例 |
| --- | --- |
| 一般直接加 `-ing` | walk → walking, look → looking |
| 以不发音的 `e` 结尾，去 `e` 加 `-ing` | shine → shining, ride → riding |
| 重读闭音节，双写末尾辅音字母加 `-ing` | sit → sitting, run → running |

### 2. There be 句型

表示"某处有某物"，`be` 的单复数与**最近的名词**一致。

- `There are some clouds in the sky.`
- `There are some boats in the river.`
- 单数：`There is a ship on the river.`

### 3. 常用介词

| 介词 | 含义 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| in | 在……里 | in the sky, in the river |
| over | 在……上方（越过） | walk over the bridge |
| under | 在……下方 | going under the bridge |

### 4. some 与 any

- `some` 用于肯定句：`There are some clouds in the sky.`
- `any` 用于否定句和疑问句：`Are there any boats in the river?`

## ✏️ 练习 Exercises

### 一、用所给动词的正确形式填空（现在进行时）

1. The sun ______ (shine) brightly today.
2. Mr. Jones and his wife ______ (look) at the boats.
3. Tim ______ (watch) the aeroplane now.
4. Sally ______ (read) a book in the room.
5. Look! The ship ______ (go) under the bridge.

**答案：**
1. is shining
2. are looking
3. is watching
4. is reading
5. is going

### 二、仿照例句改写句子

> 例：The sun shines. → **The sun is shining.**

1. They walk over the bridge.
2. The aeroplane flies over the river.
3. Sally looks at the ship.

**答案：**
1. They are walking over the bridge.
2. The aeroplane is flying over the river.
3. Sally is looking at the ship.

### 三、用 `there be` 句型完成句子

1. ______ some boats in the river. （有）
2. ______ a cloud in the sky. （有）
3. ______ not any ships on the bridge. （没有）

**答案：**
1. There are
2. There is
3. There are

## 💭 学习心得 Notes

- 现在进行时强调"此刻正在发生"，常与 `now`、`Look!`、`Listen!` 等词连用，别忘了 `be` 动词要随主语变化（I am / he, she, it is / you, we, they are）。
- 加 `-ing` 时注意去 `e`（shine→shining）和双写（sit→sitting）两类特殊情况。
- 本课反复出现方位介词 in / over / under，可与动作词搭配（walk over, go under, fly over），建议在脑中"画面化"记忆。
